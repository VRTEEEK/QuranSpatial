## 2. Lead — written, verified, logged

- `FoundationModelsLeadWriter`: one or two sentences, **default guardrails**, prompt carries the passages.
- `LeadVerifier` rejects: Arabic script; any number that is not 55 or a retrieved ayah; more than two
  sentences or sixty words; **more than 2 novel content words, or novel words above 15% of content
  words** (content word = alphabetic token of 4+ letters, not a function word, compared by a light
  stem; allowed vocabulary = the passages + the question + a fixed framing list + the surah's name).
  Threshold pinned by `LeadVerifierTests.thresholdIsTwoNovelWordsAndFifteenPercent`.
- Adversarial tests (all pass): Arabic in a lead; "Ayah 14" when 13 was retrieved; "31 times"; an
  invented explanation ("emphasise gratitude…"); Pharaoh and Moses; empty, overlong, three sentences.
- Accept/reject is recorded in `Answer.leadStatus` and logged (`Lead by foundation-models: …`).
- Report-only run on this Mac, 8 probes: **5 accepted, 2 rejected, 1 error** ("Unsupported language"
  on ayah 33, whose passages carry bracketed transliteration). Rejections were for new words such as
  "compassion", "creatures", "humans" — the verifier doing its job. Before the surah's name was added
  to the framing list the same run was 2 accepted / 5 rejected.

## 3. App wiring (visionOS) — built, not device-tested

- **Trigger:** look at the Arabic and pinch. The ayah plane now carries a collision box, an
  `InputTargetComponent` and a hover effect, and the RealityView has a `TapGesture` targeted to it
  calling `enterAsk()`. The HUD's Ask (debug) button stays.
- **Speech:** `AskSpeechSession`. `SpeechAnalyzer` + `SpeechTranscriber` (en-US, progressive preset,
  assets installed on first use) when available; otherwise `SFSpeechRecognizer` with
  `requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`. Audio via `AVAudioEngine` input tap, converted to the
  analyzer's best format. Stops on **1.5 s of silence after speech** (`SilenceDetector`, RMS < 0.012,
  unit-tested), on **Done**, after 8 s with no speech, or at 20 s. The panel shows "Listening…", the
  live partial text, then the transcript.
- **Engine:** `AskSession` sends the transcript and the pinned ayah to AskCore; the panel shows the
  decision ("From the sources" / "From the sources, in part" / "Referred to a scholar" / "Not about
  this ayah"), the optional lead, the verbatim passages with source lines, and the note. An empty
  transcript asks for the ayah's meaning, which is the baseline Meaning panel. Continue resumes.
- **Model unavailable on device:** `AskEngineBridge.status()` -> rules router, no lead, and one small
  line on the panel ("On-device model unavailable (…): rules router, no lead.").
- **AskCore is not yet linked to the app target** (a project-file change, which is yours). The app
  compiles either way: with the package absent, `#if canImport(AskCore)` falls back to the Meaning
  passage and the panel says "Ask engine not linked". The package itself builds for the visionOS
  simulator (`xcodebuild -scheme AskCore -destination 'generic/platform=visionOS Simulator'`).
