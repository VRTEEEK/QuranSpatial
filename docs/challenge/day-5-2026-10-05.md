### Mo's decisions (5 Oct, afternoon) and what was applied

1. **a18 → hadith gate patterns added:** "saying(s) of the prophet", "the prophet (said|says)",
   "prophet muhammad said", "authentic (hadith|narration|saying|report)", and `sahih` alone with a
   negative look-ahead for "Sahih International" (our translation's name). No dev item contains them;
   `sahihInternationalIsOurTranslationNotAHadithRequest` pins the exclusion.
2. **Keyword fallback after an unconfirmed or "none" model pick, on the GENERAL route only, on strong
   evidence only:** a multi-word keyword matched, or two or more distinct keywords of the same card.
   A single one-word match stays not-covered. The off-topic override is unchanged (keyword-confirmed
   model pick only). Chain entries `card-keyword-after-model-none:<id>` /
   `card-keyword-after-model-unconfirmed:<pick>-><id>`.
3. **Personal frames left as they are** (a19 "my friend…", g39 "should I be facing…" stay referred).
4. **Precedence guard for ayah questions:** before ANY redirect to general (card keyword or general
   topic), the ayah route is kept when the question shares a content word (4+ letters,
   `LeadVerifier.stem`) with the anchor ayah's translation or meaning, ignoring function words and a
   generic list (what, does, mean, about, quran, allah, lord, god, favors, deny, verse, ayah, surah …).
   Unit tests on synthetic anchors; plus `tests/eval/day5/precedence-probe.json` — **written by the
   reviewer, not blind, regression probe only** — twelve ayah questions that name a topic word or a
   card keyword and must stay meaning / answered with no card (17 "two easts and two wests" and 56
   "the maidens in paradise" were expected to possibly fail: no shared word).
5. **LeadVerifier, before the freeze:** a lead is rejected when it speaks for a source ("the Quran
   states…", "this verse says…", "Allah says…", "you are looking at"), is meta text (starts with
   "here is" / "here's", or contains "lead-in"), contains a newline or ends with "?", or copies six or
   more consecutive words from any passage (so the AI-summary box can no longer repeat the ayah's
   translation). The rejected examples in `LeadVerifierTests` are the audit's g02, g09, g10, a23 and
   run-3 g15. Fewer accepted leads is the expected and accepted outcome.

Unit tests after these changes: 79 tests, 16 suites, all passing.

**not** re-run here or later. The two fixes below address its two severe items and are measured on
the practice sets (dev, general-dev, annex12), which is all that remains to measure on.

**F1 — the repetition cue needs a pointer word.** "how many (more) times" is a high-confidence
repetition cue only when the question also carries a word that points at the screen or the refrain:
this, it, that, repeat, repeated, say, says, said, line, verse, refrain, ayah, aya, eye, question.
"how many times a day do muslims pray and when" (k56) has none, so it is no longer caught by the
cue and falls through to the general route (unit test: not-covered, no card). "how many times does
this eye repeat" and "how many more times does it say this line" still fire (unit tests).

**F2 — the off-topic override needs strong evidence.** When the model says off-topic and the picker
proposes a card, the override now accepts the pick only if a multi-word keyword of that card is in
the question, or two distinct keywords are. A single keyword never overturns an off-topic decision.
"which direction is the nearest exit in this building" (k60) stays declined (unit test); so does
"which direction is the weather coming from today" (engine test); d47 and g04 are unchanged.

### The kill at 18:40:37 — a scene-update watchdog, caused by a main-thread spin in `AskSession`

Pulled from the headset after the test (`devicectl device copy from --domain-type systemCrashLogs`;
copy in `scratch/headset-2026-10-05-watchdog.ips`, local only). Not my console job ending (it had a
two-hour allowance and reported the kill itself), not jetsam (the 19:04 jetsam event lists the second
instance as merely suspended) and not a crash in the engine:

> `0x8BADF00D` scene-update watchdog transgression: exhausted real (wall clock) time allowance of
> 10.00 seconds · Elapsed application CPU time 10.026 s · thermal state fair · main thread in
> `closure #1 in AskSession.begin(anchor:)`

10.0 s of application CPU inside a 10 s window is one core fully busy: the main thread was spinning,
not blocked. The cause is in `AskSession.begin`'s watcher loop: the `.finishing` case of its
`switch` records the speech-end time and has **no `await`**, so while the speech session is in
`.finishing` the loop runs on the main actor without ever yielding. On the SpeechAnalyzer path the
only way out of `.finishing` is `finish(...)` inside a `MainActor.run` after
`finalizeAndFinishThroughEndOfInput()` - which needs the main actor the watcher is holding. Livelock;
the watchdog ends it after 10 s. (The 800 ms fallback in `stop(reason:)` exists only for the
SFSpeechRecognizer path and would be starved the same way.)

Why three questions got through: the watcher polls every 100 ms, and the analyzer finished 30, 36
and 67 ms after `stopping` on questions 1–3, inside the poll's sleep, so the watcher never saw
`.finishing`. On question 4 it did. The odds per question are roughly even; this will recur.

**Fix, one line, not applied tonight** (Mo asked for the cause, and the video build is installed):
the `.finishing` case must sleep like `default` does (`try? await Task.sleep(for: .milliseconds(100))`),
or the `switch` should fall to a single sleep after it. Either restores a suspension point.
